From a messy SSD to living art.

Seven milestones, one guided pipeline. The catalog is the system of record; everything downstream trusts it.

01

Consolidate, non-destructively

Ingest local folders and NAS sources — HEIC, JPEG, PNG, WebP, TIFF — detect exact and perceptual duplicates with best-original recovery, and fold everything into one content-addressed store. Sources stay untouched until you approve archival.

SHA-256journaledresumable
02

Analyze, offline and deterministically

A provider-neutral engine scores technical and aesthetic quality, saliency and subjects, crop safety, color story, and pairing affinity — every signal computable on CPU and reproducible run-to-run, with no cloud in the happy path.

raw + corrupt reportedno cloud default
03

Direct the art direction

A deterministic policy engine proposes treatments — full-bleed, contain-and-matte, panoramic, square, or diptych — each with a rationale, captured in a versioned ArtDirectionManifest with explicit target overrides.

explainableversioned
04

Render, then verify

One manifest renders byte-identical output at 1080p, 4K, or any exact size in sRGB. A validator gates publishability — dimensions, profile, hash, region math, no unintended crop. Upscaling is never silent.

1080p / 4K / customvalidatedno silent upscale
05

Approve with history

Per-image approve, reject, undo, redo, and batch, with append-only history and rationale — driven from a browser-based Darkroom Bench that is keyboard- and screen-reader-first, or from a headless CLI with stable exit codes.

web UIcliWCAG AA
06

Publish to the frame, safely

Filesystem, simulator, Samsung Art Mode (canary upload, exact-ID replace, rollback) and Home Assistant coordination behind an exclusive write lease. A durable watcher ingests new photos exactly once.

leasedcanary + rollbackingest-once
07

Personalize with Taste Lens

Versioned, isolated preference profiles rerank candidates with explainable deltas, tuned by active-learning pairwise comparisons. Disable it and you get the exact baseline back. Approved output never changes.

reversiblemeasuredbaseline-isolated

Taste Lens.

Built only after the baseline is trustworthy. It learns how a person or household weights the qualities the analysis engine already extracts — it never replaces discovery, analysis, rendering, or your approval.

01

Ranking you can reverse in one click

Versioned profiles rerank candidates as a deterministic baseline plus a personal delta, each contribution explained. Disable Taste Lens and ranking returns to the exact baseline order — approved output always renders byte-identically, on or off.

versionedexplainablebaseline-isolated
02

Trained by comparing, not answering

“I prefer this one” updates the profile deterministically. Informative pairs are chosen first for sample efficiency, and every improvement is measured against a held-out set before it’s promoted.

active learninggated evidenceblend + veto
03

A discovery desk

Taste Lens Discovery feeds from a federated, outage-isolated creator catalog into an adaptive deck — likely, adjacent, and wildcard modes, artist spotlights, and a Familiar ↔ Surprising dial. Compare two works to calibrate. A denied creator’s work is never surfaced or acted on.
