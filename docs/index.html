From a messy SSD to living art.

Seven milestones, one guided pipeline. The catalog sits at the center; everything downstream trusts it.

01

Consolidate, non-destructively

Ingest local folders and NAS sources (HEIC, JPEG, PNG, WebP, TIFF), detect exact and perceptual duplicates with best-original recovery, and fold everything into one content-addressed store. Sources stay untouched until you approve archival.

SHA-256journaledresumable
02

Analyze, offline and deterministically

A provider-neutral engine scores technical and aesthetic quality, saliency and subjects, crop safety, color story, and pairing affinity. Every signal is computable on a CPU and reproducible run to run, and none of it needs a network.

raw + corrupt reportedno cloud default
03

Direct the art direction

A deterministic policy engine proposes treatments (full-bleed, contain-and-matte, panoramic, square, or diptych), each with a rationale, captured in a versioned ArtDirectionManifest with explicit target overrides.

explainableversioned
04

Render, then verify

One manifest renders byte-identical output at 1080p, 4K, or any exact size in sRGB. A validator checks dimensions, profile, hash, region math, and that nothing was cropped unexpectedly. Upscaling is never silent.

1080p / 4K / customvalidatedno silent upscale
05

Approve with history

Per-image approve, reject, undo, redo, and batch, with append-only history and rationale. Drive it from a browser-based Darkroom Bench that is keyboard- and screen-reader-first, or from a headless CLI with stable exit codes.

web UIcliWCAG AA
06

Publish to the frame, safely

Filesystem, simulator, Samsung Art Mode (canary upload, exact-ID replace, rollback) and Home Assistant coordination behind an exclusive write lease. A durable watcher ingests new photos exactly once.

leasedcanary + rollbackingest-once
07

Personalize with Taste Lens

Versioned, isolated preference profiles rerank candidates with explainable deltas, tuned by active-learning pairwise comparisons. Disable it and you get the exact baseline back. Approved output never changes.

Taste Lens.

Added only after the baseline is trustworthy. It learns how you weigh the qualities the analysis engine already extracts, and it’s reversible: turn it off and the ranking goes back to what it was. It comes in two parts, a lens that learns your eye and a discovery desk that brings you new work.

The lens · your library

Your eye, made explicit.

Taste Lens reranks your candidates and art-direction proposals the way you actually choose. Compare two photos and prefer one; the change lands as a small, explainable shift on top of the baseline order, and every move says why. This signal, this weight, this contribution.

  • Six isolated profile kinds: personal, household, room, season, guest-safe, experimental
  • Every ranking is the baseline plus a personal delta, with each contribution listed
  • Informative pairs are chosen first, and every lift is measured before it is promoted
  • Blend profiles, veto what you dislike, replay or undo any version
  • Turn it off and the exact baseline order returns; approved output stays byte-identical
Discovery · new work

A wall of work you haven’t met.

Taste Lens Discovery is a separate surface fed by a federated catalog of painters and photographers. The deck composes a feed in likely, adjacent, or wildcard modes, surfaces artist spotlights, and lets you lean the mix from familiar toward surprising. Compare two works and the result folds back into the same profile.

  • Federated and outage-isolated: a down source never breaks the deck
  • Likely / adjacent / wildcard feed, artist spotlights, a Familiar ↔ Surprising dial
  • Pairwise calibration keeps your training continuous across both tools
  • A denied creator is never surfaced or acted on; works stay signal-isolated
  • The same action returns the same result by gesture, button, or keyboard
