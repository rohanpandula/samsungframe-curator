from a messy folder to a living wall.

Seven steps, one calm pipeline. Everything downstream trusts the catalog at the center.

consolidate

clean up your library, safely

Import photos from folders and NAS drives. Find duplicates and near-duplicates, keep the best copy, and fold everything into one catalog. Your original files are never changed or deleted unless you ask.

heic · jpeg · png · webp · tiff
analyze

rank your photos, offline

An on-device engine scores every shot on sharpness, quality, composition, color, and what stands out. No cloud in the happy path.

raw + corrupt files reported, never dropped
direct

choose how each is shown

It proposes a layout per photo — full-bleed, matted, panoramic, square, or a two-photo diptych — and says why. You can override any of it.

every choice explained
render

render, then verify

One choice produces an exact 1080p or 4k image. Nothing is upscaled or cropped without you knowing, and every file is checked before it counts.

exact · verified · no silent upscale
approve

approve before it's used

Approve, reject, undo, and batch-review from a simple browser app or the command line. Full history is kept, with your reasons.

web ui · cli · keyboard-first
publish

put it on the frame, safely

Send approved art to the frame via Samsung Art Mode or Home Assistant, with backups and rollback if anything goes wrong. A watcher picks up new photos automatically.

filesystem · simulator · samsung · home assistant
personalize

let it learn your taste

Taste Lens gently reorders suggestions to match you — explainable, tuned by comparing two photos, reversible with one click. Approved output never changes.

reversible · measured · baseline-isolated

private by construction.

taste lens.

Added only after the baseline is trustworthy. It learns how you weigh the qualities the engine already extracts, and it's reversible: turn it off and the ranking goes back to what it was. Two tools live under the name.

the lens · your library

your eye, made explicit

Reranks your candidates the way you actually choose. Compare two photos and prefer one; the change lands as a small, explainable shift on top of the baseline order, and every move says why. This signal, this weight, this contribution.

  • six isolated profile kinds: personal, household, room, season, guest-safe, experimental
  • every ranking is the baseline plus a personal delta, each contribution listed
  • informative pairs chosen first; every lift measured before it is promoted
  • blend profiles, veto what you dislike, replay or undo any version
  • turn it off and the exact baseline order returns; approved output stays byte-identical
discovery · new work

a wall of work you haven't met

A separate surface fed by a federated catalog of painters and photographers. The deck composes a feed in likely, adjacent, or wildcard modes, surfaces artist spotlights, and lets you lean from familiar toward surprising. Compare two works to calibrate.

  • federated and outage-isolated: a down source never breaks the deck
  • likely / adjacent / wildcard feed, artist spotlights, a familiar ↔ surprising dial
  • pairwise calibration keeps your training continuous across both tools
  • a denied creator is never surfaced or acted on; works stay signal-isolated
  • the same action returns the same result by gesture, button, or keyboard
